Start with 1,000 virtual credits. Choose a total bet of 10, 20, 50 or 100. Each spin divides it equally across 10 fixed lines. Match 3 or more symbols from the leftmost reel. Three, four or five Ivoris icons anywhere pay 2×, 10× or 50× the total bet and award 10 free spins. Scatter wins are added to line wins. Free spins keep the triggering bet and cost no credits. Three or more icons during the bonus add another 10 spins.

At the start of the bonus, one regular symbol is chosen. If it appears on 3 or more reels during a free spin, it expands across those reels and pays on every line, even when the reels are not adjacent. Each line pays the higher of its normal win and its expansion win.
